## The lens

The deterministic layer reveals that all available evidence for PCSK9 inhibitors stems from cardiovascular event-driven trials with stringent exclusion criteria (e.g., 76% moderate kidney failure exclusion per meta-analysis), creating a critical void in longevity-specific outcomes like all-cause mortality in aging populations, thus framing any longevity thesis as an extrapolation beyond the evidence base.

## Already known — do not publish

- PCSK9 inhibitors lower LDL-C by approximately 15-60% depending on agent
- PCSK9 inhibitors reduce primary cardiovascular outcomes by HR 0.80-0.96 in trials like FOURIER and ODYSSEY
- Lp(a) levels are reduced by 25-30% with PCSK9 inhibitors as a pleiotropic effect

## Tensions / contradictions

- Trial efficacy (HR 0.80-0.96 for primary outcomes) versus high exclusion of patients >75 years (11% representation) and multimorbidity (51% excluded), skewing data away from longevity-relevant subgroups
- Observed Lp(a) reduction of 25-30% with PCSK9 inhibitors versus unclear impact on mortality, contrasting with other agents like mipomersen that reduce Lp(a) by 25-40% but have severe side effects

## Evidence gaps

- All-cause mortality data in patients over 75 years with comorbidities like moderate kidney failure, which are excluded from 76% of trials
- Long-term (>7 year) survival effects of PCSK9 inhibitors beyond 2.8-year trial periods, with no dedicated longevity endpoint studies
- Comparative effectiveness on lifespan versus SGLT2 inhibitors, which show stroke reduction (HR 0.83) but also lack mortality focus

## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- No direct evidence links PCSK9 inhibitor-induced LDL-C or Lp(a) reduction to extended lifespan; longevity claims would be speculative based on surrogate endpoints
- Trial populations are highly selected (e.g., 64% exclusion of moderate heart failure), so results may not generalize to real-world aging cohorts where longevity benefits are sought
- The absence of longevity-specific outcome data (e.g., mortality hazard ratios) makes any thesis non-falsifiable with current evidence
