## Autophagy as a mediator of exercise-induced anti-tumor effects: a scoping review of ULK1/LC3B pathways in murine models versus human biomarkers

## Why this is surprising

The differential modulation of autophagy (ULK1 phosphorylation at Ser555) and systemic inflammation (hs-CRP, sTNFαR2) by exercise presents a non-obvious mechanistic link that could reconcile its pleiotropic effects in cancer and geriatric settings, but only if dose-response and combined therapy protocols are critically examined across murine and human models.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Exercise improves physical function in elderly; Exercise reduces tumor growth in mice; Exercise decreases inflammatory biomarkers in cancer survivors

Real tension: Exercise alone reduced hs-CRP by 30.2% in cancer survivors (fact 23), while exercise combined with metformin reduced it further but with wider confidence intervals (fact 21), suggesting interaction effects that are not fully characterized
