=== DISCUSSION ===

This scoping review asked whether rapamycin or sirolimus administration extends lifespan in murine models and whether the primary-study corpus admits a formal quantitative synthesis of that effect. The automated pipeline screened [PACKET:study_selection|N_SCREENED] candidate records through deterministic pre-pass, single-LLM adjudication, evidence-contract verification, and per-quote A-core audit. [PACKET:primary_pool_composition|N_ACCEPTED] records satisfied the declared evidence contract for the primary endpoint of lifespan, and [PACKET:primary_effect|k_studies] of those carried extractable survival metrics under the contract gate. That count falls below the k ≥ 2 threshold required for the inverse-variance random-effects pool within a single metric family. No pooled effect estimate, confidence interval, or heterogeneity statistic is therefore reported. The present exercise maps the evidence landscape rather than quantifying a treatment effect.

Prior meta-analyses have quantified rapamycin's influence on murine lifespan under varying inclusion criteria and modelling strategies. Swindell (2017) [CIT:swindell-2017-meta|prior-meta-analysis] pooled survival ratios across heterogeneous dosing regimens and reported a statistically significant extension. Fok et al. (2023) [CIT:fok-2023-meta-rapamycin|prior-meta-analysis] updated that synthesis with additional records and confirmed both direction and approximate magnitude. Tyshkovskiy et al. (2025) [CIT:tyshkovskiy-2025-vertebrate-meta|prior-meta-analysis] situated rapamycin among multiple longevity interventions in mice, providing comparative effect rankings. The present scoping review's smaller contract-passing corpus reflects stricter evidence-contract and A-core audit criteria applied during automated adjudication rather than a narrower search; the retrieval sweep was multi-source and designed for high sentinel recall. Because [PACKET:primary_effect|k_studies] is below the pooling threshold, this report neither confirms nor challenges the effect magnitudes those syntheses reported. Alignment with their conclusions would require additional primary studies whose evidence quotes satisfy the contract gate's intervention, comparator, and endpoint coverage requirements.

The mechanistic rationale for rapamycin-mediated lifespan extension centres on inhibition of mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1), a conserved nutrient-sensing kinase that regulates autophagy, protein translation, and cellular senescence [CIT:lamming-2013-mtor|mechanism-review] [CIT:saxton-2017-mtor|mechanism-review]. Chronic mTORC1 suppression shifts cellular homeostasis toward maintenance and stress-resistance pathways that are plausibly linked to delayed ageing phenotypes in mouse models. These mechanistic overviews provide the biological framework within which the lifespan endpoint is interpreted; they do not themselves constitute lifespan evidence and were excluded from the primary-effect extraction pipeline by design.
