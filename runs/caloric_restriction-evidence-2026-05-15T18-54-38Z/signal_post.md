## Sexual Dimorphism in Caloric Restriction Efficacy for Intestinal Neoplasia Prevention: Evidence from Apc(1638N/+) Mouse Models

## Why this is surprising

The sex-specific and population-dependent paradoxes in caloric restriction's effects—such as opposing inflammatory responses in athletes versus obese women, and sex-dimorphic cancer outcomes in mice—reveal that CR is not a universal intervention but a context-dependent modulator, necessitating precision-based approaches over generalized recommendations.

Real tension: In Apc(1638N/+) mice, visceral fat removal attenuated macroadenomas in females but not males, while caloric restriction attenuated macroadenomas in males but not females (facts 1 and 7).
