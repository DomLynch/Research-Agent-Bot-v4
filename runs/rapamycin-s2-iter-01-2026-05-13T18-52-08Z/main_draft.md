## Methods

Search Strategy. A single automated retrieval sweep queried [PLACEHOLDER:databases] for records published through [PLACEHOLDER:date-range]. The executed query combined terms for the primary intervention (rapamycin, sirolimus) with terms for the study system (mouse, mice, murine) and the primary endpoint (lifespan, longevity, survival, lifespan extension), connected by Boolean operators; the full query string is logged in `search_query_log.json`. Duplicate records were collapsed by DOI and PMID. Reporting follows the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses framework [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation], adapted to the automated pipeline context in which retrieval, screening, and extraction are documented by machine-readable receipts rather than manual flow diagrams. The retrieval step has already executed at the time of writing; all parameters reported here correspond to the completed sweep rather than a projected protocol.

Eligibility. Inclusion and exclusion criteria are structured in PECO terms appropriate to preclinical aging research. Population: murine subjects (mouse or mice) of any strain, sex, or age at treatment initiation. Exposure: administration of rapamycin or its pharmacological synonym sirolimus, at any dose, route, or schedule. Comparator: vehicle-treated, untreated, or diet-matched control groups. Outcome: reported lifespan data in the form of median survival, mean lifespan, hazard ratio, or percent change in survival relative to control. Studies were excluded if they investigated only translational-only analogues (everolimus, RTB101, or broader rapalog classes) without a rapamycin or sirolimus arm; such records are retained in a translational sensitivity layer and are not pooled into the primary corpus. Studies were also excluded if they reported only in-vitro endpoints, used non-murine systems, or lacked any extractable survival numeric. Adjudication is performed by a single large-language-model judge operating against the universal evidence contract, with a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass that filters records by keyword match, parsed-text adequacy, and character-count floor before the LLM stage reviews the remainder. There is no second human reviewer and no third-reviewer adjudicator in this pipeline. All eligibility decisions, including accept, reject, and unclear classifications, are filed in `eligibility_receipts.json` with the contract clause that determined each outcome.

Data Extraction. From each included paper the pipeline extracts: the reported effect size (percent change in median lifespan, mean lifespan difference, or hazard ratio for survival), the sample size per arm (control n and treated n), the metric family to which the effect belongs, the rapamycin dose and schedule, the mouse strain and sex, the age at treatment initiation, the duration of observation, and a minimum of two non-title evidence quotes that anchor each extracted numeric to its source text. All numerics are recorded in their original reported units; no unit conversion is applied at the extraction stage. Extraction is performed by a single automated LLM pass per included paper under the universal evidence contract, which requires parsed-text adequacy, a character-count floor of [PLACEHOLDER:min_text_chars], endpoint coverage, and intervention-control coverage before an extraction receipt is generated. Every receipt—comprising effect estimates, evidence quotes, model metadata, and timestamps—is filed in `effect_extractions.json`. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are designed-for capabilities of the pipeline; the present iteration executes a single automated pass and defers the dual-pass reconciliation to the pre-publication step.

Moderator Coding. The following pre-specified moderators are coded from the extracted text of each included study: [MODERATOR_P:mouse_strain] (e.g., C57BL/6, HET3, heterogeneous stock, other), [MODERATOR_P:sex] (male, female, mixed, or not reported), [MODERATOR_P:age_at_initiation] (young-adult, middle-aged, late-life, or original-unit continuous), [MODERATOR_P:dose_category] (low ≤ 2 mg/kg, moderate 2–8 mg/kg, high > 8 mg/kg, or original-unit continuous), [MODERATOR_P:treatment_schedule] (continuous, intermittent, or not reported), and [MODERATOR_P:route] (diet, gavage, injection, other). Categories are derived from the original text without imposing arbitrary numeric thresholds when continuous values are reported; sensitivity to alternative binning is planned for the pre-publication step. Moderator coding follows the same single-pass extraction as effect estimates; no independent second coder has verified these fields in the present iteration.

Risk of Bias and Quality Assessment. The universal evidence contract performs an automated screen that evaluates five operational criteria for every candidate record: (1) parsed-text adequacy, meaning the PDF-to-text or XML parse produced a readable body; (2) character-count floor, ensuring sufficient substantive text to support extraction; (3) at least two non-title evidence quotes that reference quantitative results; (4) endpoint coverage, confirming the paper addresses the declared primary endpoint; and (5) intervention-control coverage, confirming the paper contrasts rapamycin or sirolimus against a comparator arm on that endpoint. Records failing any gate are demoted to an `unclear` classification. The per-domain risk-of-bias framework appropriate to preclinical animal studies is the SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool [CIT:hooijmans-2014-syrcle|method-citation], which addresses sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding, incomplete outcome data, and selective reporting in animal experiments. Formal application of this framework by domain experts is deferred to the pre-publication step; the present pipeline does not execute per-item risk-of-bias ratings beyond the contract gates described above. Reporting guidance follows the ARRIVE 2.0 recommendations for preclinical studies [CIT:percie-du-sert-2020-arrive|method-citation], with the understanding that a machine-extraction pipeline cannot verify compliance with all items but can flag relevant text for human audit.

Statistical Synthesis. The pipeline runs an inverse-variance random-effects point estimate over contract-passing extractions within a single metric family drawn from [PLACEHOLDER:preferred_metric_families]. The estimator weights each study-level effect by the inverse of its within-study plus between-study variance components, with between-study heterogeneity estimated by restricted maximum likelihood. A pooled estimate is reported only when k ≥ 2 contract-passing effects exist within the metric family; at k = 1 or k = 0 the pipeline reports the individual effect descriptively without pooling. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus—including study-ID and outcome-within-study random intercepts, the Hartung-Knapp confidence-interval adjustment [CIT:hartung-knapp|method-citation], and execution through the metafor package [CIT:viechtbauer-2010-metafor|method-citation]—is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5–10 contract-passing effects per metric family. Heterogeneity quantification via the I² statistic [CIT:higgins-2003-i2|method-citation] and prediction intervals is likewise deferred until that threshold is met; these are designed-for capabilities of the pipeline, not operations executed in the present iteration.

Sensitivity Analyses. Leave-one-out analysis, influence diagnostics (Cook's distance), funnel-plot inspection, Egger's regression for small-study bias [CIT:egger-1997-funnel|method-citation], and trim-and-fill imputation are all designed-for capabilities of the pipeline that require k ≥ 10 contract-passing effects per metric family to produce stable estimates. The present iteration does not execute any of these procedures because the corpus does not meet that threshold. A translational sensitivity layer is maintained for records excluded from the primary pool on intervention grounds: studies using everolimus, RTB101, or broader rapalog classes are mapped in a separate evidence table to support discussion of mechanistic generality across mTOR pathway inhibitors [CIT:lamming-2013-mtor|mechanism-review] [CIT:saxton-2017-mtor|mechanism-review], but their effect estimates are not pooled with the rapamycin/sirolimus primary corpus.

Tension-Matrix Construction. The pipeline includes a designed-for tension-matrix capability in which moderator combinations (e.g., strain × sex × dose × age at initiation) define matrix cells and cell-level effect estimates are compared to surface qualitative contradictions in the evidence base. Cells populated by fewer than two contract-passing effects are flagged as sparse and excluded from cross-cell comparison. The present iteration does not render this matrix because the corpus has not accrued enough moderator-stratum cells to populate it meaningfully. The capability will activate once k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects accumulate across distinct moderator strata, enabling the pipeline to generate a cell-by-cell summary that highlights convergent and divergent findings without overinterpreting sparse data.
