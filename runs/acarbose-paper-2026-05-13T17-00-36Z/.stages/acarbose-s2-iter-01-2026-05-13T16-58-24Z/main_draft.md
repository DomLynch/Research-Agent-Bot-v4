## Methods

A single automated retrieval sweep was executed across [PLACEHOLDER:databases] on [PLACEHOLDER:date-range], using a query structured around the topic's scope vocabulary (mouse, mice, murine), primary endpoint (lifespan), and eligible interventions (acarbose, alpha-glucosidase inhibitor), with Boolean connectors and controlled-vocabulary synonyms assembled into the final query string [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Duplicate records were collapsed by DOI and PMID before eligibility screening. This search-and-report procedure follows the PRISMA 2020 reporting framework for systematic reviews [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation]. The retrieval step is reported as executed rather than planned because the sweep completed before Methods was rendered; the resulting corpus of unique records constitutes the input to the eligibility stage.

Inclusion criteria were specified in PECO terms appropriate to preclinical ageing research: Population, adult mice of any strain or sex; Endpoint, lifespan (median, mean, or maximum reported as a discrete survival metric); Comparator, untreated, vehicle, or standard-diet control; Outcome, any quantified lifespan effect attributable to the declared intervention. Studies administering acarbose or other alpha-glucosidase inhibitors as the primary intervention qualified for the primary pooled corpus [CIT:percie-du-sert-2020-arrive|method-citation]. Eligibility adjudication was performed by a single LLM judge against a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass that screens for the presence of the required PECO elements before the judge evaluates contextual fit; no second human reviewer or third-reviewer adjudicator participated in this iteration. Records failing the universal evidence contract—parsed-text adequacy, minimum character-count floor specified in pack.eligibility_min_text_chars, at least two non-title evidence quotes, endpoint coverage, and intervention/control coverage—were demoted to the unclear tier. The primary pooled corpus is restricted to records whose evidence quotes confirm acarbose or alpha-glucosidase inhibitor as the primary intervention; translational-only interventions, should they appear in the retrieval set, are retained in a separate translational sensitivity lane but excluded from the primary inverse-variance pool. All adjudication decisions were filed in eligibility_receipts.json.
