## The lens

The absence of A/B-bound evidence facts on deep sleep architecture metrics (e.g., slow-wave sleep duration, delta power) prevents the formulation of a specific thesis, as the available alpha hints focus solely on sleep duration and broad health outcomes. A publishable angle would require extracting polysomnography-derived deep sleep data from cohorts in the hinted studies to isolate architecture-specific effects.

## Already known — do not publish

- Deep sleep is restorative and declines with age
- Sleep architecture involves stages like N1, N2, N3 (deep sleep), and REM
- Poor sleep quality is linked to various health issues

## Tensions / contradictions

- Alpha hints indicate long sleep duration is associated with mortality and CVD risk, but the role of deep sleep architecture (e.g., proportion or intensity) remains unquantified
- Heritability estimates for sleep duration (42-45% in adulthood) contrast with the unexplored genetic determinants of deep sleep architecture

## Evidence gaps

- The impact of deep sleep metrics on specific subgroups, such as those with obstructive sleep apnea prevalence of 50%-79% from hints
