The absence of indexed evidence facts prevents the formulation of specific, citable theses; this gap highlights the critical need for targeted data extraction on inflammaging biomarkers across age cohorts and tissue-specific senescence to enable evidence-based research framing.

## Already known — do not publish

- Inflammaging is a chronic low-grade inflammation associated with aging.
- Macrophage infiltration and senescent cell accumulation increase with age in adipose tissues.
- Pro-inflammatory cytokines like IL-6 and TNF-α are elevated in older adults.

## Tensions / contradictions

- Discrepancy between studies reporting lower pro-inflammatory status in certain aging subgroups versus the typical inflammaging paradigm.
- Inconsistent findings on senescent cell density between visceral white adipose tissue (vWAT) and subcutaneous white adipose tissue (scWAT).

## Evidence gaps

- Quantitative prevalence of inflammaging in octogenarians beyond the 40% threshold reported in isolated studies.
- Functional role of CD68+ macrophages in different adipose depots and their direct link to inflammaging severity.
- Longitudinal data on how tissue-specific senescent cell dynamics evolve with age and contribute to systemic inflammation.
