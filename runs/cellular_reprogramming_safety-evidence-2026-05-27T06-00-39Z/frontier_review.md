## The lens

With no cited evidence facts on cellular reprogramming safety, the evidence pool is too narrow for a publishable thesis. Key gaps exist in understanding vector-specific toxicology (e.g., AAV-based systems), age-dependent safety profiles, and long-term oncogenic risks, which are critical for translating reprogramming technologies.

## Already known — do not publish

- Cellular reprogramming carries inherent risks such as tumorigenesis
- Reprogramming efficiency is a primary focus in research
- Safety concerns are acknowledged in the field

## Tensions / contradictions

- Discrepancy between promising lifespan extension data in aged mice using AAV-OSK systems and lack of corresponding safety evaluations
- Contrast in safety assessments for reprogramming across different tissues, such as kidney where RCC is prevalent but safety data is sparse

## Evidence gaps

- Long-term safety and immune response of inducible OSK delivery via AAV in 124-week-old male mice models
- Safety implications of reprogramming in cancer-prone tissues like kidney, given the high incidence of clear cell RCC
- Dose-response relationships for reprogramming factors in elderly cohorts
