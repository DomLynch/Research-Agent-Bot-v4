## Sex-Stratified Efficacy of Caloric Restriction in Intestinal Cancer: Evidence from Genetic Mouse Models

## Why this is surprising

The sexually dimorphic response to caloric restriction in intestinal tumorigenesis challenges universal dietary interventions, revealing that CR's anti-cancer efficacy is mechanistically tied to sex-specific pathways, necessitating a shift toward personalized, sex-stratified nutritional strategies.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Caloric restriction reduces body weight and improves insulin sensitivity; Dietary restriction extends lifespan in model organisms; CR lowers inflammatory markers like hs-CRP and IL-6

Real tension: CR reduces macroadenomas in male Apc(1638N/+) mice but not in females, while visceral fat removal is effective only in females (facts 31457 vs 31458)

## Evidence

- In females, macroadenomas were markedly attenuated by VF- (1.33 ± 0.23 mean ± SE; P < 0.05), but not by caloric restriction (2.35 ± 0.25; P = 0.71), as compared with ad libitum (2.50 ± 0.34). **[1.33]** (Cancer Prevention Research 2013)
- In males, however, caloric restriction (1.71 ± 0.26; P < 0.01), but not VF- (2.94 ± 0.42; P = 0.29), reduced macroadenomas, as compared with ad libitum males (3.47 ± 0.30). **[1.71]** (Cancer Prevention Research 2013)
