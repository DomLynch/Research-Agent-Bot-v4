- **Population:** human WIL2-NS cells
- **Intervention:** 30 nmol/L folic acid (folate deficiency)
- **Source:** *Folate Deficiency Induces Dysfunctional Long and Short Telomeres; Both States Are Associated with Hypomethylation and DNA Damage in Human WIL2-NS Cells* — Cancer Prevention Research (2013)
  · DOI: `10.1158/1940-6207.capr-13-0264`
- **Validator:** researka-tier2

- **Why it matters:** Folic acid deficiency in cell cultures leads to 26% longer telomeres, suggesting nutritional factors directly influence telomere maintenance and potential aging pathways.
- **Caution:** This study used a single human cell line (WIL2-NS) with fixed doses, limiting direct applicability to whole-organism or varied human contexts.
- **Next question:** How does folic acid deficiency impact telomere length in human tissues in vivo, and what are the implications for aging-related diseases?
