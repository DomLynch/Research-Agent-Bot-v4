## Already known — do not publish

- Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets and osteomalacia
- Supplementation with 400 IU/day prevents rickets
- Vitamin D levels correlate with bone turnover and osteoporosis risk

## Tensions / contradictions

## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- Insufficient direct evidence from randomized trials to link vitamin D supplementation to extended healthspan outcomes
- Reliance on observational data for COVID-19 associations introduces confounding biases
- Heterogeneity in dose regimes (200-2000 IU/day) and population baselines complicates meta-analysis
