===METHODS===

The retrieval process was executed as a single automated sweep across [PLACEHOLDER:databases] for the period [PLACEHOLDER:date-range] using the query string [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Deduplication was performed via DOI and PMID matching. The reporting of this search and the subsequent flow of records follows the guidelines specified by [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation]. To ensure the inclusion of sentinel papers that the automated retrieval cannot reach, manual full-text injections were performed; these records were SHA-256-hashed and recorded as side-car files to maintain the integrity of the retrieval set.

Eligibility was adjudicated by a single LLM judge against a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass, with all decisions filed in `eligibility_receipts.json`. Inclusion required studies to utilize the murine study system, specifically mouse or mice, with the primary endpoint of lifespan. The primary pooled corpus was restricted to the interventions rapamycin and sirolimus. Translational-only interventions, including everolimus, rtb101, and rapalogs, were explicitly excluded from the primary pooled analysis but retained as a translational sensitivity layer to map analog evidence. Records were required to meet a universal evidence contract, which mandated parsed-text adequacy, a character count exceeding [PLACEHOLDER:eligibility_min_text_chars], and the presence of at least two non-title evidence quotes confirming endpoint, intervention, and control coverage.

Data extraction was performed by a single automated LLM pass per included paper, with all numerics, evidence quotes, model names, and timestamps filed in `effect_extractions.json`. The pipeline extracted effect sizes, sample sizes, and metrics in their original reported units. A strict A-core gate was applied to each record, requiring per-quote evidence that the experiment utilized the declared murine system, primary intervention, control/comparator, and lifespan endpoint; records failing this gate were demoted to B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes in `primary_effect_input_set_strict.json`. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are deferred to the pre-publication step.

Moderators were pre-specified based on the topic scope to allow for the partitioning of variance. These included [MODERATOR_P:dosage], [MODERATOR_P:administration-schedule], and [MODERATOR_P:age-of-onset]. Extraction was performed using original units to avoid the imposition of arbitrary numeric thresholds, allowing for post-hoc category sensitivity analysis. The rationale for these moderators is based on the known pharmacological variability of rapamycin in murine models [CIT:lamming-2013-mtor|mechanism-review].

The pipeline performed an automated quality screen via the universal evidence contract, verifying parsed-text adequacy, character-count floors, and the presence of at least two non-title evidence quotes covering the intervention, control, and endpoint. While the [CIT:percie-du-sert-2020-arrive|method-citation] framework is the declared anchor for the study designs in this domain, per-domain expert risk-of-bias adjudication using that framework is deferred to the pre-publication step.

Statistical synthesis is implemented as an inverse-variance random-effects pool over contract-passing extractions within a single metric family. A point estimate is reported only when k ≥ 2. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus, including REML between-study variance estimation, the Hartung-Knapp adjustment [CIT:hartung-knapp|method-citation], and execution via the metafor package [CIT:viechtbauer-2010-metafor|method-citation], is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5–10 contract-passing effects per metric family.

Sensitivity analyses, including leave-one-out diagnostics, influence diagnostics, funnel-plot inspection, Egger's regression [CIT:egger-1997-funnel|method-citation], and trim-and-fill, are deferred until k ≥ 10. Quantification of heterogeneity via I² [CIT:higgins-2003-i2|method-citation] and prediction intervals are similarly deferred until this threshold is met. A translational sensitivity layer was maintained for everolimus and rapalogs to provide an analog-evidence map without pooling these results into the primary murine lifespan corpus.

Tension-matrix construction is a designed-for capability of the pipeline where moderator combinations define cells to identify conflicting effect directions across strata. Sparsely populated cells are flagged to indicate low evidence density. The present iteration does not execute this analysis as the corpus has not yet accrued enough moderator-stratum cells; the matrix is deferred until k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects accumulate across moderator strata.
