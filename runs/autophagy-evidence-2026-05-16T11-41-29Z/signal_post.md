## LncRNA-mediated autophagy as a prognostic dichotomy in lung adenocarcinoma versus glioblastoma therapy resistance

## Why this is surprising

Autophagy's context-dependent modulation by lncRNAs and ubiquitin tagging reveals divergent prognostic and therapeutic implications across pathologies, challenging the notion of a uniform autophagic response. This framing shifts focus from generic autophagy activation to specific molecular arbitrators that could enable targeted interventions in cancer and infectious disease.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Cytoprotective autophagy enhances cancer cell survival during therapy; Selective autophagy targets intracellular pathogens like Mtb for clearance; Autophagy-related lncRNAs serve as biomarkers in lung adenocarcinoma

Real tension: The strong prognostic association of autophagy-related lncRNAs in lung adenocarcinoma (HR 1.215-1.256) [7110,7112] contrasts with the cytoprotective role of autophagy in glioma therapy resistance [8399], indicating cancer-type-specific autophagy roles that may inform tailored therapeutic strategies.
