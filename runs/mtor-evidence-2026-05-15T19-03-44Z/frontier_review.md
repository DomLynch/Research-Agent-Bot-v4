# Frontier review — mtor

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-15T19-03-44Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

mTOR's therapeutic manipulation presents a Janus-faced dilemma: its inhibition bolsters waning immunity in the elderly yet may inadvertently fuel metastatic pathways in cancer, demanding a precision approach that current systemic treatments fail to provide.

## Already known — do not publish

- mTOR pathway regulates cell growth and metabolism
- mTOR inhibitors are used as immunosuppressants and anticancer agents
- mTOR hyperactivation is common in various cancers

## Tensions / contradictions

- RAD001 improves influenza vaccine response in elderly humans (2014) vs. mTOR hypomorphism causing 40% mortality in mice (2013), highlighting immune dependency
- Torin2's potent mTOR inhibition (EC50 250 pmol/L) but off-target effects on DNA-PK, ATR, ATM (2013), complicating therapeutic specificity
- mTOR hyperactivation in 70% of urothelial carcinomas (2022) vs. mTOR pathway activation linked to liver metastasis in breast cancer (2017), suggesting organ-specific roles

## Evidence gaps

- Lack of studies on mTOR inhibitor dosing regimens optimized for immune enhancement in aged cancer patients
- Underexplored combination of mTOR inhibitors with DNA repair-targeting agents based on off-target effects
- Limited data on mTOR modulation in neurological disorders like epilepsy with NPRL3 variants

## Paper theses

### #1 — opportunity 86 · `scoping-review`

**Thesis:** Contextual mTOR Modulation: Age and Tissue-Specific Therapeutic Windows for Immune and Cancer Applications

- novelty 80 / evidence_strength 70 / reviewer_risk 65
- **Why publishable:** Synthesizes disparate findings on mTOR's roles in aging immunity and cancer metastasis to propose a precision framework for geriatric oncology, leveraging high-impact studies like the RAD001 vaccine trial from Science Translational Medicine (2014).


## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- The proposed therapeutic windows are inferred from non-overlapping studies; direct evidence in elderly cancer patients is needed.
- Scoping reviews may lack the depth of systematic reviews or meta-analyses, potentially limiting impact.

## Suggested next extractions

- Preclinical models testing Torin2 analogs with improved selectivity for mTOR vs. DNA repair kinases
