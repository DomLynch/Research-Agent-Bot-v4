# Frontier review — immunosenescence

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-27T05-47-44Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

The evidence base lacks direct, quantified links between specific immune cell subsets and concrete clinical endpoints in defined aging subpopulations. Current literature is fragmented across immunology, geriatrics, and vaccinology without synthesizing dose-response relationships or mechanistic thresholds for intervention.

## Already known — do not publish

- Immunosenescence contributes to vaccine inefficacy in the elderly
- Inflammatory markers increase with aging
- CVID patients exhibit immunosenescence features

## Tensions / contradictions

- High-cited mechanistic reviews (e.g., Frontiers in Immunology 2017) lack granular clinical outcome data, while clinical studies (e.g., Drugs & Aging 2024) lack mechanistic immunophenotyping.
- Ecology Letters 2019 cross-species inflammation data vs. human-specific T-cell senescence timelines in Aging Cell 2023.

## Evidence gaps

- No data linking CD8+CD28- T-cell percentages or CD4/CD8 ratios to specific respiratory infection hospitalization rates in community-dwelling adults over 70.
- Missing dose/exposure thresholds for how much CMV serostatus or lifetime antigen burden shifts immune risk profile toward frailty.
- No studies comparing immunosenescence biomarker kinetics (e.g., TREC levels) across race/ethnicity subgroups in response to vaccination.

## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- The proposed evidence gap is trivial—immune aging is well-characterized; you merely demand more data without a novel hypothesis.
- Your 'gap' ignores existing cohort studies linking immunosenescence phenotypes to mortality (e.g., Leiden Longevity Study), making your premise invalid.
- Without specifying which of the 100+ proposed senescence markers to prioritize, your 'next extractions' are unactionable.

## Suggested next extractions

- Quantified CD8+CD28- cell percentages vs. influenza hospitalization rates in adults >70 from large EHR-linked biobanks.
- Longitudinal TREC and CD31+ recent thymic emigrant data in Black vs. White participants in the Health ABC Study.
- CMV serostatus, IgG titers, and multi-morbidity counts correlated with response to high-dose vs. adjuvanted flu vaccine in nursing home residents.
