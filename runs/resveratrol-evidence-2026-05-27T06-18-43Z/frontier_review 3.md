## The lens

The singular in vitro evidence for resveratrol's 80% viral titer reduction against HCoV-229E creates a stark contrast with the broader, unsupported therapeutic claims in the literature, framing it as a case study in how isolated mechanistic findings propagate without clinical substantiation.

## Tensions / contradictions

- The high-dose tolerance (up to 1 g/day from alpha hints) contrasts with the lack of dose-response data in the viral study

## Evidence gaps

- dose-response studies for antiviral effects beyond 80% reduction in HCoV-229E
- mechanistic pathways linking viral inhibition to known signaling like PI3K/Akt/Nrf2 from alpha hints
