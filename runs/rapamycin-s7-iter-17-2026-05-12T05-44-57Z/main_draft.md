## Results

### Study Selection
Of 502 records identified through systematic database search, 502 were screened at title/abstract level; 298 were flagged as candidate records for full-text retrieval. Open-access full-text availability was located for 257 candidates; full-text content was parsed for 139 of these. Pre-specified eligibility adjudication was applied to 140 records, yielding 22 auto-eligible records (universal evidence contract passed), 108 excluded, 9 flagged for manual review of low-confidence or rule-judge conflicts, and 1 documented unavailable (retrieval / parser gap); the subset of strict A-core records is the canonical input for primary-effect extraction [PACKET:study_selection].

### Sentinel Recall Audit
