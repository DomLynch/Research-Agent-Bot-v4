===METHODS===

Search Strategy. The retrieval sweep was executed across [PLACEHOLDER:databases] on [PLACEHOLDER:date-range], using a query architecture built around the core construct "carbon tax" with domain-specific extensions for emission outcomes, economic indicators, and policy evaluation study designs [CIT:page-prisma-2020|method-citation]. The query shape combined Boolean operators with field tags targeting title, abstract, and keyword metadata, producing [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Deduplication was performed by DOI and PMID matching prior to eligibility adjudication. The full executed query string, hit counts per source, and deduplication log are preserved in the retrieval receipts file. This retrieval constitutes a single automated sweep; iterative query refinement or snowball citation chasing is not a component of the present pipeline iteration.

Eligibility. Inclusion criteria were structured in PECO terms appropriate to policy-evaluation research: Population comprised jurisdictions implementing or simulating carbon pricing; Exposure (Intervention) was a carbon tax instrument, restricted in the primary pooled corpus to [PLACEHOLDER:primary_interventions]; Comparator was a counterfactual scenario (no tax, baseline trajectory, or alternative policy); Outcome encompassed emissions, economic output, energy consumption, or distributional effects reported as quantifiable effect sizes. Exclusion criteria applied to studies lacking parseable numeric results, those examining only carbon cap-and-trade without a tax component, and pure opinion or advocacy pieces. [PLACEHOLDER:translational_only_interventions] were excluded from the primary pool but retained as a translational sensitivity layer. Adjudication was performed by a single LLM judge operating against a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass that screens for study-design keywords, outcome-type markers, and intervention-class tags before the judge evaluates nuanced eligibility [CIT:kellner-llm-screening-2024|method-citation]. There is no second human reviewer and no third-reviewer adjudicator within this pipeline. All adjudication decisions—accept, reject, or unclear—are filed in eligibility_receipts.json with the rule-triage outcome and judge rationale. The universal evidence contract gates all accepted records: parsed text must be adequate, character count must exceed [PLACEHOLDER:eligibility_min_text_chars], at least two non-title evidence quotes must be present, and endpoint coverage and intervention/control coverage must be demonstrable. Records failing any contract criterion are demoted to the unclear lane.

Data Extraction. From each contract-passing record, the pipeline extracted the following variables in their original reported units: effect size (point estimate and direction), sample size or observation count, outcome metric and unit, intervention specification, comparator specification, study design type, geographic jurisdiction, temporal scope, model or estimator name, and a minimum of two verbatim evidence quotes linking the reported numeric to the declared endpoint and intervention [CIT:polanin-prisma-2020|method-citation]. Extraction is performed by a single automated LLM pass per included paper; every extraction receipt—including timestamps, raw numerics, evidence quotes, and model metadata—is filed in effect_extractions.json. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication of extraction disagreements are deferred to the pre-publication step; at present, the pipeline relies on the evidence-contract gate and the A-core audit (described below) to enforce extraction fidelity. The strict A-core per-quote audit further demotes records whose evidence quotes do not affirm the topic pack's declared study system, primary intervention, control/comparator, and endpoint, routing them to B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes documented in primary_effect_input_set_strict.json.

Moderator Coding. Pre-specified moderators were drawn from the topic pack's scope and coded from the original extraction variables without post-hoc reclassification into arbitrary numeric bins [CIT:bourguignon-decomposition-2001|method-citation]. Moderators include: [MODERATOR_P:tax_rate_level] (the carbon price per unit in original currency and year, coded as a continuous variable), [MODERATOR_P:jurisdiction_type] (national, subnational, or supranational), [MODERATOR_P:study_design] (quasi-experimental, computable general equilibrium, simulation/modeling, or other), [MODERATOR_P:sector_coverage] (economy-wide, energy sector only, industrial, transportation), [MODERATOR_P:temporal_horizon] (short-run ≤5 years, medium-run 6–15 years, long-run >15 years), and [MODERATOR_P:outcome_family] (emissions, economic output, energy, distributional). Original-unit extraction is maintained; category-level sensitivity is planned as a post-hoc robustness layer. No numeric thresholds are imposed that would conflate continuous variation with categorical bins prior to inspection of the extracted distribution.

Risk-of-Bias and Quality Assessment. The automated universal evidence contract performs a baseline quality screen comprising: parsed-text adequacy verification, character-count floor enforcement, confirmation of at least two non-title evidence quotes, endpoint coverage validation, and intervention/control coverage validation [CIT:sterne-robins-i-2016|method-citation]. These contract criteria function as a minimum-threshold gate rather than a domain-specific quality appraisal. The topic pack's method-citation anchor list declares the appropriate per-domain risk-of-bias framework for the quasi-experimental and policy-evaluation designs that characterize the carbon-tax evidence base; that framework is [CIT:robins-i-anchor|method-citation]. Per-domain expert adjudication applying this framework to each included study is deferred to the pre-publication step and is not executed within the present automated iteration. The pipeline's contract gate ensures that only records with sufficient textual evidence for downstream verification enter the pooled corpus.

Statistical Synthesis. The pipeline runs an inverse-variance random-effects pool over contract-passing effect extractions within a single metric family drawn from [PLACEHOLDER:preferred_metric_families], reporting a pooled point estimate and 95 percent confidence interval at k ≥ 2 [CIT:borenstein-introduction-2009|method-citation]. The between-study variance component is estimated by the DerSimonian-Laird method within this lightweight pool. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus—random intercepts for study ID and outcome-within-study, REML estimation of between-study variance, Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman adjustment for small-study confidence intervals, and implementation via metafor [CIT:viechtbauer-metafor-2010|method-citation]—is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5 to 10 contract-passing effects within a single metric family. This threshold ensures sufficient degrees-of-freedom estimation for the moderator structure. The present iteration reports only the unadjusted inverse-variance point estimate when the k-threshold condition is met.

Sensitivity Analyses. Leave-one-out influence analysis, formal influence diagnostics (Cook's distance analogs for meta-analytic leverage), funnel-plot inspection, Egger's regression for small-study bias, trim-and-fill imputation, and heterogeneity quantification (I², prediction intervals, τ²) are all deferred until k ≥ 10 contract-passing effects accumulate within a metric family [CIT:egger-bias-1997|method-citation] [CIT:duval-trimfill-2000|method-citation]. These capabilities are designed-for within the pipeline architecture; the present iteration does not execute them because the anticipated carbon-tax corpus may not supply sufficient independent effects at first pass. If the topic pack lists translational-only interventions (e.g., simulated carbon taxes in laboratory or stated-preference settings), an analog-evidence map is prepared for those records without pooling them into the primary corpus, enabling cross-domain inference without violating effect-family homogeneity.

Tension-Matrix Construction. The tension matrix is a designed-for capability of the pipeline intended to visualize and quantify disagreements across moderator-stratum cells—for example, whether short-run emission effects diverge between economy-wide and sector-specific taxes at comparable price levels [CIT:bourguignon-decomposition-2001|method-citation]. Cells are defined by the cross-product of two or three key moderators (e.g., jurisdiction type × sector coverage × outcome family); sparsely populated cells (k < 2) are flagged rather than interpolated. The present iteration does not render the tension matrix because the corpus has not yet accrued enough contract-passing effects distributed across moderator strata to populate the matrix at a minimum density threshold. Construction is planned once k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects accumulate across the relevant moderator cross-classification.
