## Why this is surprising

Beclin 1's autophagy-independent regulation of tight junction permeability via occludin endocytosis represents a paradigm shift in epithelial barrier dynamics, challenging the canonical view of autophagy proteins and revealing untapped therapeutic targets for diseases like inflammatory bowel disease.
