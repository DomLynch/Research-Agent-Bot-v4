## Population-specific glycemic responses to fasting: A meta-analysis stratifying by diabetes status and intervention type (Ramadan vs. intermittent fasting)

## Why this is surprising

The collected evidence reveals a pronounced paradox: fasting ameliorates glycemic and metabolic parameters in healthy populations but may worsen them in diabetics, pointing to a critical need for pathology-stratified guidelines rather than universal recommendations. Furthermore, animal studies on gut permeability (e.g., in Ross chickens) hint at unexplored mechanistic pathways that could inform human conditions like NAFLD, but translational gaps persist.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Fasting decreases fasting blood sugar in healthy men; Ramadan fasting reduces BMI and insulin in healthy subjects; Fasting lowers blood pressure in long-term programs
