## One-sentence thesis

## Why this is surprising

The sexually dimorphic and dose-dependent longevity effects of rapamycin in mice underscore a complex pharmacology where transient versus continuous mTOR inhibition interacts with genetic background and sex-specific aging pathways, challenging uniform anti-aging assumptions.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Rapamycin extends lifespan in laboratory mice; mTOR inhibition is a key mechanism in aging and longevity; Rapamycin is a common geroprotector in preclinical studies

Real tension: Bitto 2016's transient rapamycin in C57BL/6 mice (60% extension) contrasts with ITP's continuous feeding in UM-HET3 mice showing 14-26% extension, suggesting strain and regimen dependencies.
