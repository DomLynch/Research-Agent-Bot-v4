# Frontier review — klotho

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-27T09-33-02Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

The 80% reduction in serum klotho from nephron-specific deletion in mice, contrasted with modest odds ratios (0.78-0.85) for kidney decline in human cohorts, suggests that soluble klotho may not be the primary systemic mediator in age-related kidney disease, pointing to compensatory mechanisms or alternative pathways.

## Already known — do not publish

- Klotho deletion in nephrons reduces serum levels
- Higher soluble klotho is associated with slower eGFR decline in older adults

## Tensions / contradictions

- The near-complete elimination of serum klotho in Six2-KL(-/-) mice versus the modest, sometimes non-significant, odds ratios in the Health Aging and Body Composition study
- The incident rate ratio of 0.90 with confidence interval crossing 1 (0.78 to 1.04) indicates uncertainty in the human data

## Evidence gaps

- The role of klotho in arterial stiffness and hypertension, as hinted by a 45% reduction in affected patients
- Molecular mechanisms of klotho shedding, specifically the impact of amino acid deletions like residues 954-962 on the 94% shedding reduction
