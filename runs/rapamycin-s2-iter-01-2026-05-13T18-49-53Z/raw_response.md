===METHODS===

The retrieval process was executed as a single automated sweep across [PLACEHOLDER:databases] for the period [PLACEHOLDER:date-range] using the query [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Deduplication was performed via DOI and PMID matching. The reporting of this search and the subsequent flow of records follow the guidelines specified in [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation]. For sentinel papers that the automated retrieval could not reach, manual full-text injections were performed, with each record SHA-256-hashed and stored as a side-car to ensure provenance.

Eligibility was adjudicated by a single LLM judge against a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass, with all decisions filed in `eligibility_receipts.json`. Inclusion required studies to utilize the murine study system, with the primary endpoint defined as lifespan. The primary pooled corpus was restricted to interventions using rapamycin or sirolimus. Translational-only interventions, including everolimus, rtb101, and rapalogs, were explicitly excluded from the primary pooled analysis but retained as a translational sensitivity layer. Records were required to meet a universal evidence contract, including parsed-text adequacy, a character-count floor of [PLACEHOLDER:eligibility_min_text_chars], and the presence of at least two non-title evidence quotes covering both the endpoint and the intervention/control arms.

Data extraction was performed by a single automated LLM pass per included paper, with all numerics, evidence quotes, model names, and timestamps filed in `effect_extractions.json`. Extracted variables included the effect size, sample sizes, and the specific metric in original reported units. A strict A-core audit was applied to each quote to verify that the experiment utilized the declared murine system, primary intervention, control/comparator, and endpoint; records failing this audit were demoted to B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes in `primary_effect_input_set_strict.json`. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are deferred to the pre-publication step.

Moderators were pre-specified based on the topic pack scope to allow for variance partitioning. These included [MODERATOR_P:dose], [MODERATOR_P:administration-schedule], and [MODERATOR_P:age-of-onset]. Extraction was performed in original units to allow for post-hoc category sensitivity analysis, avoiding the application of arbitrary numeric thresholds during the initial automated pass.

The pipeline performed an automated quality screen via the universal evidence contract, verifying parsed-text adequacy, character-count floors, and the presence of at least two non-title evidence quotes. While the [CIT:percie-du-sert-2020-arrive|method-citation] framework provides the appropriate standard for the study designs in this domain, per-domain expert risk-of-bias adjudication using that framework is deferred to the pre-publication step.

Statistical synthesis is implemented as an inverse-variance random-effects pool over contract-passing extractions within a single metric family. A point estimate is reported only when k ≥ 2. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus, including REML between-study variance estimation, the Hartung-Knapp adjustment [CIT:hartung-knapp|method-citation], and execution via the metafor package [CIT:viechtbauer-2010-metafor|method-citation], is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5–10 contract-passing effects per metric family.

Sensitivity analyses, including leave-one-out diagnostics, influence diagnostics, funnel-plot inspection [CIT:egger-1997-funnel|method-citation], and trim-and-fill, are deferred until k ≥ 10. Quantification of heterogeneity via I² [CIT:higgins-2003-i2|method-citation] and the calculation of prediction intervals are similarly deferred. A translational sensitivity layer was maintained to map analog evidence from translational-only interventions without pooling them into the primary murine corpus.

Tension-matrix construction is a designed-for capability of the pipeline, where moderator combinations define cells to identify contradictory evidence strata. Sparsely populated cells are flagged to indicate low evidence density. The present iteration does not execute this rendering because the corpus has not yet accrued enough moderator-stratum cells; the matrix is deferred until k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects accumulate across moderator strata.
