## MiMo's note

Sarcopenia prevalence is not a single number but a cascade of diagnostic components (low mass, low strength, low performance) whose relative frequency shifts dramatically by comorbidity — dialysis patients show 26.2% severe sarcopenia vs 3.0% in non-dialysis, while NAFLD progression correlates with skeletal muscle depletion from ~8-22% in controls to ~35-63% in NASH — yet most meta-analyses collapse these into a single pooled estimate, obscuring the mechanistic pathway from muscle quantity to muscle function to disability. The publishable framing is not 'prevalence varies' but rather 'the diagnostic cascade breaks at different points depending on disease context, meaning intervention targets must shift accordingly.'
