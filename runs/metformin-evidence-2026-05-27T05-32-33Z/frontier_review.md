## The lens

Metformin's efficacy is strikingly context-dependent: it shows robust survival benefits in WHO grade III gliomas and acute infection mortality in diabetic patients, yet fails in grade IV gliomas, suggesting disease-specific biological mechanisms that could guide precision repurposing.

## Already known — do not publish

- Metformin's primary role in glycemic control for type 2 diabetes
- Standard use of metformin versus insulin in gestational diabetes management

## Tensions / contradictions

- Significant survival benefit in WHO grade III glioma (HR 0.30 for OS) versus no benefit in grade IV glioma (HR 0.83 for OS)
