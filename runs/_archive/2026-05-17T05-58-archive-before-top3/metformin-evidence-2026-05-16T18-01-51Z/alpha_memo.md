## One-sentence thesis

Context-Dependent Efficacy of Metformin in Cancer Prevention: A Scoping Review Dissecting Preclinical-Clinical Discrepancies

## Why this is surprising

The paradoxical attenuation of metformin's anti-cancer promise in population-level studies, despite potent preclinical efficacy, implicates unappreciated modulators such as metabolic context, dosing regimen, or combinatorial interventions that may define its true therapeutic niche.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Metformin's established role as a first-line antidiabetic agent for type 2 diabetes; General anti-proliferative effects of metformin observed in laboratory cancer models

Real tension: Tension between metformin's 72% tumor burden reduction in NNK-induced A/J mice [1] and null association with colorectal cancer risk in a large diabetic cohort [2]
