## The lens

The juxtaposition of mTOR deficiency-induced immunological mortality and metformin-mediated mTOR inhibition in tumor suppression reveals a therapeutic paradox where pathway modulation's efficacy is contingent on host resilience and disease context, challenging one-size-fits-all approaches.

## Already known — do not publish

- mTOR regulates cell growth and metabolism
- mTOR inhibitors are used in cancer therapy

## Tensions / contradictions

- Hypomorphic mTOR mice exhibit 40% mortality (fact 15567) highlighting immune fragility, while metformin's mTOR inhibition reduces tumor burden by 72% (fact 2848) in carcinogen-exposed mice, indicating a trade-off between survival and oncogenic control.

## Evidence gaps

- No comparative studies on mTOR modulation doses in immunocompromised versus tumor-bearing models
- Lack of data on metformin's effects in mTOR-deficient genetic backgrounds
- Underexploration of temporal dynamics of mTOR inhibition across different disease stages
