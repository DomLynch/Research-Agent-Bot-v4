# Frontier review — metformin

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-16T16-13-59Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

Framing metformin's anti-cancer potential through the paradox of robust preclinical efficacy at high doses versus inconsistent clinical epidemiology, highlighting dose-response relationships, combination strategies, and patient metabolic phenotypes as critical translational determinants.

## Already known — do not publish

- Metformin reduces overall cancer incidence and mortality in diabetic patients (facts 1110, 1111)
- Metformin is an established glucose-lowering agent with weight loss effects (facts 63845, 17869)
- Metformin activates mTOR inhibition in preclinical models (fact 2848)

## Tensions / contradictions

- Preclinical inhibition of colorectal cancer PDX tumors at 150 mg/kg metformin (fact 35442) versus no epidemiological association in a large diabetic cohort (fact 11416)
- Metformin monotherapy alone shows no significant effect on inflammatory markers in cancer survivors (fact 22120) versus significant reduction when combined with exercise (facts 22118, 22119)

## Evidence gaps

- Efficacy of metformin in non-diabetic cancer patients at human-relevant doses, as most evidence derives from diabetic populations
- Synergistic effects of metformin with boswellic acid nanoparticles (fact 32860) in human colorectal or pancreatic cancer trials
- Role of mTOR inhibition (fact 2848) as a predictive biomarker for metformin response in human tumors
