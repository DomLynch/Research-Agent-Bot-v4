## One-sentence thesis

Sexual Dimorphism in NAD Redox State as a Predictor of Host Susceptibility to NAD-Capped Bacterial Pathogens

## Why this is surprising

The coexistence of sex-dependent NAD redox ratios in healthy humans and NAD-capped RNA in bacterial pathogens points to an unexplored axis where host metabolic sex differences may influence microbial virulence mechanisms through shared NAD-dependent pathways.

Known / obvious (do not republish): NAD is a central cofactor in mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation; NAD+ levels are known to decline with age and metabolic stress

Real tension: Human plasma NAD+/NADH ratios exhibit significant sex-based variation (fact 25459), yet bacterial NAD capping (fact 17507) is studied in isolation from host physiological factors like sex, creating a methodological gap in translational research
