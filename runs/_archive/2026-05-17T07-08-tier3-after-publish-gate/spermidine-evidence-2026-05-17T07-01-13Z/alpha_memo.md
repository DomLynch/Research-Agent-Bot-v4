## One-sentence thesis

Genetic Determinants of Spermidine-Mediated Yield Enhancement in Indica Rice: Implications for Targeted Polyamine Biofortification

## Why this is surprising

The stark disparity in yield improvement between KDML105 (62%) and Pokkali (16%) rice under identical spermidine pretreatment highlights a genotype-specific polyamine utilization efficiency, which remains uncharacterized but could be a key target for precision breeding, contrasting with the conserved substrate specificity seen in mammalian polyamine synthases.

Known / obvious (do not republish): Spermidine pretreatment increases rice grain yield per plant; Spermine synthase shows high specificity for spermidine over analogs

Real tension: The 46-percentage-point difference in yield enhancement between KDML105 and Pokkali rice cultivars subjected to the same 1 mM spermidine pretreatment in a 2013 study
