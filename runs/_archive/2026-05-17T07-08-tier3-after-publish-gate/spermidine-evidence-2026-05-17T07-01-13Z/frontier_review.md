## The lens

The stark disparity in yield improvement between KDML105 (62%) and Pokkali (16%) rice under identical spermidine pretreatment highlights a genotype-specific polyamine utilization efficiency, which remains uncharacterized but could be a key target for precision breeding, contrasting with the conserved substrate specificity seen in mammalian polyamine synthases.

## Already known — do not publish

- Spermidine pretreatment increases rice grain yield per plant
- Spermine synthase shows high specificity for spermidine over analogs

## Tensions / contradictions

- The 46-percentage-point difference in yield enhancement between KDML105 and Pokkali rice cultivars subjected to the same 1 mM spermidine pretreatment in a 2013 study
- The marked reduction in enzymatic activity (down to 4-17% of spermidine rates) for structural analogs like sym-homospermidine and N-(3-aminopropyl)-cadaverine in rat prostatic spermine synthase assays

## Evidence gaps

- Optimal spermidine concentrations and application timings for diverse indica rice cultivars beyond KDML105 and Pokkali
- Genetic or molecular mechanisms underlying the cultivar-specific response to exogenous spermidine in plants
