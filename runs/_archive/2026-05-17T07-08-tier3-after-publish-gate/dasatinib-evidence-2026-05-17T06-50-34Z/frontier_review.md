## The lens

Dasatinib's 50% efficacy rate in DLBCL cell lines masks a binary response pattern that likely reflects uncharacterized molecular subtypes, while its senolytic properties (hinted in aging studies) propose a dual role in hematologic malignancies and age-related pathways, but the evidence is too fragmented for a cohesive thesis.

## Already known — do not publish

- Dasatinib is a multikinase inhibitor
- Dasatinib shows efficacy in 50% of DLBCL cell lines

## Tensions / contradictions

- The 50% efficacy in DLBCL cells contrasts with near-universal resistance in solid tumors, yet no resistance mechanisms are identified in the available data

## Evidence gaps

- DLBCL cell line subtypes (e.g., ABC vs. GCB) not stratified for dasatinib response
- Lack of in vivo or clinical data on dasatinib for DLBCL
- No integration of dasatinib's senolytic effects with lymphoma biology
