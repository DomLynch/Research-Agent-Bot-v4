Scoping Review of Fisetin's Context-Dependent Bioactivity: Bridging Anti-Oncogenic and Antimicrobial Evidence Gaps

## Why this is surprising

Fisetin's significant tumor growth inhibition at 30 mg/kg in vivo contrasts sharply with its µg/mL-range antimicrobial efficacy in vitro, suggesting that its bioactivity is highly context-dependent and potentially mediated by divergent or shared pathways that have not been systematically compared.

Known / obvious (do not republish): fisetin inhibits tumor growth by 35.7% in nude mice at 30 mg/kg; fisetin and myricetin inhibit S. mutans growth by 81% and 86% at 8 µg/mL

Real tension: Divergent effective concentrations: 30 mg/kg (intraperitoneal, in vivo) for tumor inhibition vs. 8 µg/mL (in vitro) for bacterial inhibition, raising questions about bioavailability and target specificity.
