## One-sentence thesis

The Compliance Paradox in Fasting: When Patient Autonomy Overrides Medical Safety in Diabetes Management

## Why this is surprising

Fasting is not a uniform intervention but a context-dependent modulator of health, where identical practices yield therapeutic nutritional benefits in cancer cohorts while precipitating acute metabolic crises in non-compliant diabetic populations, revealing a critical tension between patient autonomy and clinical safety.

## What would weaken this

- The evidence pool is too heterogeneous, with varying populations, interventions, and outcomes, limiting generalizability.
- Sample sizes in cited studies appear small, and confounding factors (e.g., cancer treatment, diabetes management) are not adequately controlled.
- The proposed thesis on compliance paradox relies heavily on Ramadan-specific data, which may not translate to other fasting contexts.

## Next extraction
