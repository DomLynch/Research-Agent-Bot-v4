## Results

### Study Selection
Of 485 records identified through systematic database search, 485 were screened at title/abstract level; 158 were flagged as candidate records for full-text retrieval. Open-access full-text availability was located for 107 candidates; full-text content was parsed for 56 of these. Pre-specified eligibility adjudication was applied to 56 records, yielding 5 auto-eligible records (universal evidence contract passed), 48 excluded, 3 flagged for manual review of low-confidence or rule-judge conflicts; the subset of strict A-core records is the canonical input for primary-effect extraction [PACKET:study_selection].
