=== DISCUSSION ===

The present scoping review evaluates the effect of metformin on lifespan in murine models. The analysis is designed to determine if metformin consistently extends lifespan across diverse mouse strains and conditions, utilizing a corpus of [PACKET:primary_pool_composition] studies. The capacity of the corpus to support a definitive conclusion depends on the number of contract-passing effects identified in [PACKET:primary_effect]. If [PACKET:primary_effect] contains k < 2, the analysis cannot pool effects, and the results will be presented as individual study observations.

These planned evaluations extend prior syntheses that have addressed the longevity-promoting properties of biguanides. Previous meta-analyses have identified varying degrees of lifespan extension in mice [CIT:chen-2022-metformin-meta|prior-meta-analysis] [CIT:ivimey-cook-tyshkovskiy-2025-meta|prior-meta-analysis]. The present work is designed to align with or diverge from these findings by applying a strict evidence-contract gate to ensure that only records with explicit endpoint and intervention coverage are included. Where the current corpus size is small, the analysis will describe alignment with prior reports without asserting a superior estimate.

The biological basis for these effects is generally attributed to the activation of adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase (AMPK) and the subsequent inhibition of complex I in the mitochondrial respiratory chain [CIT:madiraju-2014-metformin-mechanism|mechanism-review]. Further mechanistic reviews suggest that metformin modulates nutrient sensing and insulin signaling pathways to improve metabolic homeostasis [CIT:foretz-viollet-2014-mechanism|mechanism-review]. These mechanisms provide the theoretical framework for the observed lifespan extensions in murine models, though the specific contribution of each pathway to longevity remains a subject of active investigation.

Several boundary conditions remain underdetermined within the current murine corpus. The influence of mouse strain, sex, dosage, administration timing, and route of delivery on the magnitude of lifespan extension is not yet fully quantified. Additionally, the interaction between metformin and the pathogen status of the mice is a variable that requires further stratification. The present analysis is designed to map these variables, but the power to detect significant moderator effects is contingent upon the final count of contract-passing studies.

Translational considerations are addressed by examining the gap between murine findings and human outcomes. Clinical trials have explored metformin for age-related comorbidities, though the direct translation of lifespan extension from mice to humans remains unproven [CIT:crowley-2017-metformin-clinical|clinical-trial]. It is explicitly noted that translational-only interventions, such as buformin and phenformin, are excluded from the primary pooled analysis to maintain the purity of the metformin-specific effect estimate. These biguanides are retained only for sensitivity layers or discussion of chemical class effects.

=== LIMITATIONS ===

The primary limitation of this review is the potential for restricted corpus size, as the sentinel-recall gate [PACKET:sentinel_recall] may exclude studies not indexed in the primary retrieval sweep. The statistical power of the synthesis is constrained by the number of studies meeting the evidence contract in [PACKET:primary_effect]; if k is low, the ability to generalize the effect of metformin across murine populations is limited. Furthermore, the pipeline enforces strict metric-family discipline, meaning 90th-percentile lifespan ratios and median lifespan ratios are pooled separately, preventing cross-family inference. Risk-of-bias adjudication is performed by a single LLM judge without human arbitration, which may introduce systematic errors in quality assessment. Finally, manual full-text injections [PACKET:study_selection] were used only for source recovery of sentinels and did not serve as eligibility overrides. The absence of granular data on dose-response curves and sex-stratified outcomes in several primary records limits the precision of the planned moderator analysis.

=== CONCLUSION ===

The current corpus supports a calibrated estimate of metformin's effect on murine lifespan, contingent upon the number of contract-passing studies identified in the primary pool. The strength of this conclusion would be modified by the inclusion of additional high-evidence records or the successful repair of sentinel-recall gaps. This work provides a structured evidence-contract framework to refine the understanding of biguanide-mediated longevity in mice, serving as a basis for future translational hypotheses.
