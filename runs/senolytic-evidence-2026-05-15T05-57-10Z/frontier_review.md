# Frontier review — senolytic

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-15T05-57-10Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

The paradoxical role of senolytics in bone marrow adiposity (BMAT) expansion and endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndoMT) reveals a tissue-specific efficacy trade-off, where senescent cell clearance may inadvertently promote lipogenesis in irradiated bone while destabilizing vascular plaques in atherosclerosis models.

## Already known — do not publish

- Senolytics reduce senescent cell burden
- Aging is associated with senescent cell accumulation
- SASP factors contribute to tissue dysfunction

## Tensions / contradictions

- ABT-263 at 100 mg/kg or 50 mg/kg reduced smooth muscle cells by 90% but caused >50% mortality in advanced atherosclerotic Apoe-/- mice (JCI Insight 2024)
- D+Q treatment decreased miR34a (P = 0.016) yet increased p53 gene expression (P = 0.041) in aging mice (Aging 2020)
- BMAT was upregulated at day 7 post-radiation, but senolytic D+Q effects were observed at day 42, suggesting timing-dependent interactions (Journal of Bone and Mineral Research 2020)

## Evidence gaps

- The dose-response relationship of ABT-263 on EndoMT across different atherosclerosis stages
- The impact of senolytics on BMAT in human subjects aged >70 years with senescent cell prevalence >50%
- The role of irisin as a mediator between senolytic therapy and bone mineral density in clinical populations

### #2 — opportunity 51 · `evidence-gap`

**Thesis:** Senolytic-Induced EndoMT as a Hidden Risk Factor in Atherosclerotic Plaque Destabilization

- novelty 80 / evidence_strength 45 / reviewer_risk 70
- **Why publishable:** Drawing from JCI Insight 2024 data on ABT-263 increasing EndoMT by 60% in Apoe-/- mice, this angle challenges the assumption that senolytics uniformly improve vascular health and could inform targeted drug design to avoid off-target effects.


## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- The high mortality rate (>50%) in ABT-263-treated Apoe-/- mice may stem from model-specific factors like western diet or genetic background, limiting translational relevance to human senolytic trials
- The correlation between irisin serum levels and femoral BMD (R = 0.619) in older adults is observational and does not demonstrate causality for senolytic interventions
- Evidence strength for senolytic effects on BMAT is limited to a single murine study at 42 days post-radiation, requiring longer-term and multi-model validation

## Suggested next extractions

- Dose-titration studies of ABT-263 in early versus advanced atherosclerosis models to assess EndoMT thresholds
- Temporal analysis of BMAT changes in human bone biopsies following senolytic therapy for osteoporosis
- Clinical measurement of irisin levels in senolytic trials to evaluate its potential as a biomarker for bone health outcomes
