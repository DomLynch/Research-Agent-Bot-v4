Threshold Paradigm in Longevity: Bridging Human Genetic Enrichment and Seed Moisture Breakpoints

## Why this is surprising

Longevity mechanisms exhibit domain-specific critical thresholds – the 16.5% moisture breakpoint in rice seed viability and the discrete 30% mortality reduction from genetic enrichment in human offspring – suggesting that longevity is governed by nonlinear pivotal points rather than continuous gradients, a cross-biological insight with implications for aging research.
