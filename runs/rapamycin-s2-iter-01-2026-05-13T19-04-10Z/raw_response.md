===METHODS===

A single automated retrieval sweep was executed across [PLACEHOLDER:databases] with records collected through [PLACEHOLDER:date-range]. The query combined controlled-vocabulary and free-text terms targeting rapamycin (including the approved synonym sirolimus), murine lifespan or survival endpoints, and the study-level design (chronic dosing in aging cohorts) [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Duplicate records were removed by DOI and PMID matching. The search strategy is reported in accordance with the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses extension for search reporting [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation]. Because retrieval preceded manuscript drafting, the strategy documents the executed query rather than a planned protocol.

Eligibility criteria followed a PECO framework adapted to preclinical longevity research. Population: aging mouse cohorts of any genetic background. Exposure: chronic or intermittent administration of rapamycin or sirolimus at any dose, by any route. Comparator: vehicle-treated, untreated, or ad libitum-fed age-matched controls. Outcome: any reported lifespan or survival metric (median survival, mean lifespan, hazard ratio, survival probability at a defined time point, or Kaplan–Meier distribution). The primary pooled corpus is restricted to studies evaluating rapamycin or sirolimus; studies employing everolimus, RTB101, or other rapalogs are retained in a translational sensitivity layer but excluded from primary effect pooling [CIT:arriola-apelo-2016-review|narrative-review]. Adjudication was performed by a single large-language-model judge operating against a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass that enforces the universal evidence contract: parsed-text adequacy, a character-count floor of [PLACEHOLDER:char-count-threshold], at least two non-title evidence quotes drawn from the body text, and explicit coverage of the lifespan endpoint and the intervention-versus-control comparison. Records failing any contract clause were demoted to an "unclear" lane. A subsequent strict A-core gate verified that evidence quotes in each surviving record confirmed the declared study system (murine), primary intervention (rapamycin or sirolimus), control/comparator, and endpoint (lifespan). Records failing the A-core gate were filed in B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes documented in [PLACEHOLDER:primary_effect_input_set_strict_json]. Neither dual-reviewer screening nor third-reviewer adjudication was performed; these are deferred to the pre-publication step.

From each contract-passing record, the pipeline extracted the following variables in original reported units: first author and year, mouse strain or genetic model, intervention protocol (drug name, dose, route, duration, and age at treatment initiation), comparator identity, sample sizes per arm, the reported lifespan metric, and at least two supporting evidence quotes. Extraction was executed by a single automated pass; every numeric value, evidence quote, model identifier, and timestamp was filed in [PLACEHOLDER:effect_extractions_json]. A Researka Tier-2 canonical-fact cross-check overlay was applied to every extraction containing percent-change numerics; verdicts (matched, discrepant, no_canonical_fact, or no_receipt_numerics) were recorded in [PLACEHOLDER:extraction_crosscheck_json]. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are not performed in the current iteration; they are deferred to the pre-publication step.

Pre-specified moderator variables were coded from each included study to structure the narrative comparison and to prepare the data set for future meta-regression once the corpus reaches sufficient size. The moderators are: [MODERATOR_P:intervention_dose] (rapamycin or sirolimus dose in mg/kg or ppm), [MODERATOR_P:dosing_regimen] (continuous daily, intermittent, or drug-in-food), [MODERATOR_P:sex] (male, female, or mixed), [MODERATOR_P:strain] (e.g., C57BL/6, HET3, UM-HET3, genetically heterogeneous stock), [MODERATOR_P:age_at_initiation] (age in months when treatment commenced), and [MODERATOR_P:route_of_administration] (oral gavage, diet admixture, intraperitoneal injection). All categories preserve original reported values; no arbitrary numeric binning was imposed post hoc. Sensitivity of category assignments is planned for examination once the moderator-stratum cell count permits meaningful comparison.

The universal evidence contract provides an automated transparency and reporting-adequacy screen requiring adequate parsed text, a character count above the minimum threshold, at least two non-title evidence quotes verifiable against the source document, and explicit endpoint and intervention-control coverage. These clauses function as a reporting filter rather than a formal risk-of-bias instrument.
