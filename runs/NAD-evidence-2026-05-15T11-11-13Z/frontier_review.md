## The lens

The convergence of sex-specific NAD+ trajectories with age-related decline and disease-specific depletion patterns (e.g., in ArLD and diabetes) suggests a hormonal-regulatory layer on NAD+ metabolism that could explain variable supplementation outcomes and necessitate sex- and age-tailored interventions.

## Already known — do not publish

- NAD+ is a central coenzyme in cellular metabolism
- NAD+ levels decrease with aging
- Nicotinamide riboside (NR) and nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) are NAD+ precursors that elevate NAD+ levels
