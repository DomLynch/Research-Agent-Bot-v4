# Frontier review — caloric_restriction

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-16T14-18-25Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

The evidence uncovers a sexual dimorphism paradox: caloric restriction selectively inhibits intestinal adenomas in male but not female Apc-mutant mice, while simultaneously triggering uniform metabolic and proteomic shifts in both sexes, challenging the assumption of gender-agnostic dietary benefits.

## Already known — do not publish

- Caloric restriction universally reduces body weight and improves metabolic health markers
- CR is a well-established intervention for extending lifespan in model organisms

## Tensions / contradictions

- Fact 31457 shows visceral fat removal attenuates macroadenomas in female Apc(1638N/+) mice, but caloric restriction does not, while fact 31458 demonstrates the opposite sex-specific pattern for CR's efficacy
- Fact 3314 indicates CR dramatically elevates bone marrow adiposity in male C57Bl/6J mice, conflicting with its potential bone-protective effects suggested by lowered leptin and IGF-1 in fact 3312

## Evidence gaps

- Interaction effects between caloric restriction and visceral fat removal in both sexes simultaneously
- Long-term skeletal health outcomes of CR in female mouse models
- Role of sex hormones in mediating CR's divergent anti-tumor effects

## Paper theses

### #1 — opportunity 86 · `scoping-review`

**Thesis:** Sexual Dimorphism in Caloric Restriction Efficacy for Intestinal Adenoma Prevention: Mechanistic Insights from Apc-Mutant Mice

- novelty 80 / evidence_strength 65 / reviewer_risk 60
- **Why publishable:** This review would consolidate the striking sex-specific responses to CR in cancer models, arguing for mechanistic studies on hormonal or adipose-tissue pathways to inform gender-tailored cancer prevention strategies.


## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- The evidence for sex-specific cancer effects relies on a single 2013 study using Apc(1638N/+) mice, which may not generalize to other cancer models or human populations
- Cited facts cover disparate outcomes (cancer, bone metabolism, proteomics) with different mouse strains and interventions, complicating coherent synthesis
