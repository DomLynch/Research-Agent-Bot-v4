# Frontier review — NAD

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-15T08-53-15Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

The central unstated paradox in NAD+ research is that the salvage pathway enzyme NAMPT — the rate-limiting bottleneck for NAD+ biosynthesis — is itself downregulated by 14% upon chronic nicotinamide riboside supplementation (fact 22, J Physiol 2019), creating a negative feedback ceiling that no interventional trial has acknowledged or designed around. This self-limiting mechanism interacts with two other uncontrolled variables: the sex-specific baseline NAD+/NADH ratio (1.33 women vs. 1.09 men, converging only after midlife; facts 1, 6) that no supplement trial has stratified for, and the steep severity-dependent NAD+ depletion gradient in alcohol-related liver disease (432 vs. 616 μM in normals, further dropping to 322 μM in severe steatohepatitis; facts 2, 14) where the strongest mechanistic rationale for repletion exists yet zero interventional data do.

## Already known — do not publish

- NAD+ is an essential coenzyme involved in redox reactions
- NAD+ levels decline with age
- NR and NMN are NAD+ precursors that can raise NAD+ levels
- NAD+ plays a role in DNA repair and sirtuin activity
- Supplementation with NAD+ precursors has shown benefits in animal models

## Tensions / contradictions

- Fact 22 (NAMPT decreased 14% after 12 weeks of NR 1000mg 2x/d in obese insulin-resistant men, J Physiol 2019) directly contradicts the implicit assumption in fact 3 (NR 500mg 2x/d raises NAD+ in healthy older adults, Aging Cell 2022) that precursor supplementation has a monotonic dose-response — the salvage pathway feedback may impose a tissue-specific ceiling
- Fact 9 (1 week NR 1000mg/d does nothing for skeletal muscle signaling in 8 young males, J Physiol 2021) versus fact 3 (NR 1000mg/d for unspecified longer duration alters NEV biomarkers in older adults, Aging Cell 2022) leaves the temporal threshold for efficacy entirely undefined and confounded by age
- Facts 1 and 6 establish women have 22% higher NAD+/NADH ratios than men pre-menopause, yet the only exercise-metabolism trial (fact 9) enrolled exclusively 8 males, and fact 22 studied only men — the sex-age interaction on precursor response is completely unexamined
- Fact 12 (Nampt 9.8-fold overexpression in ovarian serous adenocarcinoma, 2010) suggests NAD+ salvage pathway upregulation is a cancer vulnerability, yet fact 22 shows NR itself decreases NAMPT — raising the unasked question of whether precursor supplementation could paradoxically starve NAMPT-dependent tumors
- Facts 14 and 15 show NAD+ depletion tracks steatohepatitis severity (462 vs. 322 μM, P<0.01) and correlates with myeloperoxidase (r²=0.31) and bilirubin (r²=-0.127) in ArLD, yet no NR/NMN trial has targeted this population despite the strongest dose-response rationale

## Evidence gaps

- No study has measured the time-course of NAMPT protein suppression during NR titration — the 14% decrease at 12 weeks (fact 22) could be the beginning of a deeper trough or a stable plateau, and this determines the entire long-term efficacy narrative
- No interventional NAD+ precursor trial has stratified by sex or measured sex-specific pharmacokinetics despite a 22% baseline ratio difference (facts 1, 6) that likely modulates both needed dose and achievable ceiling
- Alcohol-related liver disease with steatohepatitis (facts 2, 14, 15) represents the disease state with the most quantified NAD+ deficit and the most inflammatory correlate data, yet zero clinical trials of NAD+ precursors exist in this population
- The 1-week NR intervention in young fit males (fact 9) is the only exercise-metabolism result available, yet the dose-duration is far too short given that cellular NAD+ pool remodeling likely requires weeks — the field is drawing negative conclusions from a temporally inadequate design
- No study has tested whether NMN's anti-acetylation effects on Cyp3a25 (fact 25, aged mouse liver, Cells 2022) translate to drug-metabolism phenotypes — a clinically actionable endpoint given that aged patients are also the main NR/NMN consumer demographic

**Thesis:** Sex-stratified NAD+ metabolism as a missing variable in precursor supplementation trial design: evidence for dose-personalization

- novelty 72 / evidence_strength 50 / reviewer_risk 25

### #3 — opportunity 97 · `scoping-review`

**Thesis:** NAMPT feedback suppression as a mechanistic ceiling on chronic nicotinamide riboside efficacy: a scoping review of the salvage pathway negative feedback paradox

- novelty 85 / evidence_strength 40 / reviewer_risk 35
- **Why publishable:** Only one study (Walker et al., J Physiol 2019) has directly measured NAMPT protein change during NR supplementation, finding a 14% decrease; framing this as a pathway-level self-limiting mechanism that intersects with cancer NAMPT dependence (85% PDA overexpression, fact 20) creates a novel, testable review thesis not present in any existing NAD+ precursor review.


## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- The NAMPT suppression finding (fact 22) comes from a single study of 20 obese insulin-resistant men — extrapolating to a 'paradox' is overfitting one result into a general mechanism without replication
- The sex-difference in NAD+/NADH ratios (facts 1, 6) is from cross-sectional data in a single 205-person cohort with no interventional arm, making it impossible to know whether this difference translates to differential precursor responsiveness
- ArLD tissue NAD+ measurements (facts 2, 14, 15) are from a single center (Hepatology Communications 2020), and the correlation coefficients with myeloperoxidase (r²=0.31) and bilirubin (r²=-0.127) are modest — building a therapeutic rationale on these alone is premature
- No meta-analysis or dose-response synthesis is possible because the interventional studies use different precursors (NR vs. NMN), different doses (500-2000 mg/d), different durations (1 week to 12 weeks), and different outcome measures (NEV biomarkers vs. whole-body metabolism vs. hippocampal pools) — the evidence is too heterogeneous for quantitative synthesis
- The 8-male exercise study (fact 9) being used as a negative control is underpowered by any standard; citing it as evidence against NR efficacy is a straw-man argument

## Suggested next extractions

- NAMPT mRNA and protein expression changes at multiple timepoints during NR/NMN supplementation titration (days 1, 7, 14, 28, 84) — no study has temporal resolution on the feedback loop
- Sex-stratified NAD+ metabolome data from any interventional NR or NMN trial — even post-hoc subgroup analyses would be novel
- Hepatic NAD+ concentration before and after NR supplementation in ArLD patients, stratified by steatohepatitis presence — the single most motivated missing clinical trial
- Circulating NAD+ precursor levels (NR, NMN, NAM) as pharmacokinetic variables in women versus men at matched doses — no sex-PK data exist
- Nampt expression in NQO1-overexpressing pancreatic cancer cell lines treated with NR — tests whether precursor supplementation exploits or undermines the tumor's NAD+ dependency
