# Frontier review — therapeutic_plasma_exchange

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-26T19-50-19Z

## The lens

Current therapeutic plasma exchange research is narrowly focused on acquired TTP with adjunctive drugs like caplacizumab, leaving its efficacy in other autoimmune conditions such as thyroid storm critically underexplored, which impedes evidence-based protocol standardization.

## Already known — do not publish

- Caplacizumab reduces TPE days in aTTP by 33.3% vs placebo
- TPE is standard care for acquired TTP

## Tensions / contradictions

- The tension between invasive TPE procedures and the emergence of targeted biologics like caplacizumab in aTTP management

## Evidence gaps

- TPE dosing and outcomes in refractory thyroid storm
- Comparative effectiveness of TPE across diverse autoimmune diseases
- Lack of biomarker-driven TPE protocols
