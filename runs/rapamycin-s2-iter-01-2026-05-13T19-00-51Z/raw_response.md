=== METHODS ===

The retrieval process was executed as a single automated sweep across [PLACEHOLDER:databases] for the period [PLACEHOLDER:date-range] using the query [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Deduplication was performed via DOI and PMID identifiers. The reporting of the search and selection process follows the framework described by [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation]. For sentinel papers that the automated retrieval could not reach, manual full-text injections were performed, with each document SHA-256-hashed and recorded as a side-car to ensure provenance.

Eligibility was adjudicated by a single LLM judge against a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass, with all decisions filed in `eligibility_receipts.json`. Inclusion required studies using murine models (mice) where the primary endpoint was lifespan. The primary pooled corpus was restricted to interventions using rapamycin or sirolimus. Translational-only interventions, including everolimus, rtb101, and rapalogs, were explicitly excluded from the primary pooled analysis and retained only for a translational sensitivity layer.

Data extraction was performed by a single automated LLM pass per included paper under the universal evidence contract, with all numerics, evidence quotes, model names, and timestamps filed in `effect_extractions.json`. Extracted variables included effect sizes, sample sizes, and metrics in their original reported units. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are deferred to the pre-publication step.

Moderators were pre-specified based on the topic scope and extracted in original units to allow for post-hoc category sensitivity without arbitrary numeric thresholds. These include [MODERATOR_P:dosage], [MODERATOR_P:administration-schedule], and [MODERATOR_P:age-at-start]. The rationale for these moderators is to partition variance based on the timing and intensity of mTOR inhibition.

The pipeline performed an automated quality screen via the universal evidence contract, which required parsed-text adequacy, a character-count floor of [PLACEHOLDER:eligibility_min_text_chars], at least two non-title evidence quotes, and confirmed coverage of both the endpoint and the intervention/control arms. A strict A-core gate further demoted records to B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes in `primary_effect_input_set_strict.json` if evidence quotes did not affirm the declared study system, primary intervention, control, and endpoint. Per-domain expert risk-of-bias adjudication using the framework described by [CIT:percie-du-sert-2020-arrive|method-citation] is deferred to the pre-publication step.

Statistical synthesis is implemented as an inverse-variance random-effects pool over contract-passing effects within a single metric family. A point estimate is reported only when k ≥ 2. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus, including REML between-study variance estimation, the Hartung-Knapp adjustment [CIT:hartung-knapp|method-citation], and execution via the metafor package [CIT:viechtbauer-2010-metafor|method-citation], is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5–10 contract-passing effects per metric family.

Sensitivity analyses, including leave-one-out diagnostics, influence diagnostics, funnel-plot inspection [CIT:egger-1997-funnel|method-citation], trim-and-fill, and the quantification of heterogeneity via I² [CIT:higgins-2003-i2|method-citation], are deferred until k ≥ 10. A translational sensitivity layer is maintained to map analog evidence from translational-only interventions without pooling them into the primary murine lifespan corpus.

Tension-matrix construction is a designed-for capability of the pipeline where moderator combinations define cells to identify contradictory effect directions across strata. Sparsely populated cells are flagged to indicate low evidence density. The present iteration does not render the matrix as the corpus has not yet accrued enough moderator-stratum cells; this analysis is deferred until k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects accumulate across moderator strata.
