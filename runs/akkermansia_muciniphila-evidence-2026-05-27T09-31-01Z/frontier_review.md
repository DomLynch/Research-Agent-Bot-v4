## The lens

The absence of formally bound evidence facts limits the ability to cite specific studies, yet the alpha hints reveal a context-dependent paradox: post-antibiotic A. muciniphila exacerbates colitis-associated cancer while its pasteurized form alleviates visceral hypersensitivity, suggesting that microbial state and host pathology critically modulate outcomes.

## Already known — do not publish

- General probiotic benefits for intestinal barrier integrity

## Tensions / contradictions

- Post-antibiotic replenishment of A. muciniphila worsened CAC tumorigenesis (hint from 10.3389/fmicb.2022.932047) versus pasteurized A. muciniphila reducing colonic hypersensitivity in IBS (hint from 10.1080/19490976.2023.2298026)
- A. muciniphila abundance increased with metformin in T2D (hint from 10.3389/fendo.2020.573891) but correlated with artificial sweeteners in HCC-cirrhosis (hint from 10.1128/msystems.00153-20)
- Post-antibiotic A. muciniphila gavage damaged intestinal barrier (hint from 10.3389/fmicb.2022.932047) while A. muciniphila supplementation ameliorated colitis in LCD-fed mice (hint from 10.1080/19490976.2020.1730149)

## Evidence gaps

- Optimal timing of A. muciniphila administration relative to antibiotic use in preventing CAC promotion
- Context-specific effects of A. muciniphila on short-chain fatty acid and bile acid metabolism in oncological versus metabolic diseases
