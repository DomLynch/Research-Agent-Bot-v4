# Frontier review — melatonin_aging

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-26T19-18-12Z
**Strategist model:** mimo-v2.5-pro

## The lens

The evidence pool for melatonin's role in aging is critically fragmented, with available hints spanning plant stress responses, ribosome gene networks, and human SARS-CoV-2 epidemiology, but no direct aging-specific experimental or clinical facts to anchor a mechanistic or therapeutic thesis.

## Already known — do not publish

- melatonin is a pineal hormone regulating circadian rhythms
- melatonin levels decline with age
- melatonin has general antioxidant properties

## Tensions / contradictions

- discrepancy between high-dose (200 μM) plant studies showing stress resilience and low-dose (5-25 mg/day) human anti-inflammatory effects
- conflicting evidence models: SARS-CoV-2 risk reduction in subgroups (e.g., African Americans) versus absence of direct aging biomarker data
- in vitro ribosome-related gene modulation versus in vivo aging phenotypes

## Evidence gaps

- no direct studies on melatonin's impact on age-related cellular senescence markers in humans
- lack of dose-response data in elderly populations for aging outcomes (e.g., cognitive decline, sarcopenia)
- unexplored timing of supplementation (e.g., lifelong vs. late-life) in aging models

## Paper theses

_none_

## Reviewer objections to anticipate

- any proposed thesis would rely on indirect evidence from non-aging contexts (e.g., SARS-CoV-2, plant biology), making extrapolations to human aging speculative and unfounded
- the absence of controlled trials or mechanistic studies in aged cohorts precludes a publishable claim

## Suggested next extractions

- extract data on melatonin's effects on age-related inflammatory biomarkers (CRP, TNF, IL-6) in clinical trials with elderly participants
- harvest studies on melatonin's influence on ribosome biogenesis or free radical scavenging in aged cell lines or animal models
- investigate epidemiological data linking long-term melatonin use to longevity or age-related disease incidence
