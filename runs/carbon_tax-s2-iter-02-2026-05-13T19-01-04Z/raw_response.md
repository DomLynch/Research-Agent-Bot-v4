=== METHODS ===

The retrieval sweep was executed across [PLACEHOLDER:databases], collecting records from inception through [PLACEHOLDER:date-range]. The query combined controlled-vocabulary and free-text terms addressing carbon pricing, carbon taxation, emission outcomes, and economic welfare measures, structured as [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms]. Deduplication proceeded by DOI and PMID collision. The sweep and its reporting conform to the PRISMA 2020 framework [CIT:page2021-prisma], with the PRISMA flow diagram generated automatically from retrieval receipts at compile time. Where sentinel papers were unreachable by automated retrieval, full-text documents were manually injected and SHA-256-hashed, with the hash recorded as a side-car entry to ensure provenance traceability.

Studies were eligible if they reported the quantitative effect of a carbon tax intervention on a measurable environmental or economic outcome in a defined population or jurisdiction, following PICO/PECO framing appropriate to policy evaluation. Inclusion in the primary pooled corpus required that the intervention match the topic pack's declared primary intervention class [PLACEHOLDER:primary_interventions]. Studies examining only translational or analog pricing instruments (e.g., cap-and-trade without a direct tax component, hypothetical willingness-to-pay surveys) were retained in a translational sensitivity layer but excluded from primary pooling [PLACEHOLDER:translational_only]. Adjudication was performed by a single LLM judge against the universal evidence contract, preceded by a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass that screened for PICO element presence and basic structural eligibility. The LLM judge resolved borderline cases not triaged by the deterministic rules. All eligibility decisions—pass, fail, or demoted to unclear—were filed in eligibility_receipts.json. No second human reviewer or third-reviewer adjudicator participated in screening decisions.

The universal evidence contract imposes five sequential gates on every candidate record: (1) parsed-text adequacy, confirming that extractable prose exists beyond metadata; (2) character-count floor above [PLACEHOLDER:eligibility_min_text_chars]; (3) at least two non-title evidence quotes directly supporting numeric claims; (4) endpoint coverage matching at least one of the topic pack's declared outcome categories; and (5) intervention and comparator coverage, confirming that the study describes the tax intervention and a reference condition. Records failing any gate were demoted to unclear status. The strict A-core per-quote audit additionally verified that each evidence quote affirms the topic pack's declared study system, primary intervention, control or comparator, and endpoint simultaneously. Records whose quotes did not satisfy all four A-core elements were demoted to B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes, documented in primary_effect_input_set_strict.json.

Each included study yielded effect-size estimates in their original reported units, along with the associated variance or confidence interval, sample size or simulation-run count, metric name, outcome direction, the evidence quotes anchoring each numeric claim, intervention and comparator descriptions, jurisdiction, and study design classification. Extraction was performed by a single automated LLM pass per paper under the universal evidence contract; every receipt—numerics, evidence quotes, model identifiers, and timestamps—was filed in effect_extractions.json. A Researka Tier-2 canonical-fact cross-check overlay ran on every extraction containing percent-change numerics, verifying extracted values against known canonical benchmarks; verdicts (matched, discrepant, no_canonical_fact, or no_receipt_numerics) were filed in extraction_crosscheck.json. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are designed-for capabilities of the pipeline, deferred to the pre-publication step.

Pre-specified moderators were drawn from the topic pack's scope variables to enable structured heterogeneity exploration. [MODERATOR_P:tax_level] captures the carbon tax rate in reported units (USD per tonne CO2-eq or local-currency equivalent). [MODERATOR_P:jurisdiction] codes the implementing country, region, or subnational polity. [MODERATOR_P:sector_coverage] distinguishes economy-wide from sector-specific tax implementations. [MODERATOR_P:revenue_recycling] categorizes the revenue-use mechanism, such as lump-sum rebate, corporate tax offset, or green infrastructure investment. [MODERATOR_P:baseline_carbon_intensity] records the pre-intervention emissions intensity of the jurisdiction or sector. [MODERATOR_P:study_design] classifies the methodological approach (e.g., computable general equilibrium, difference-in-differences, input-output analysis). All moderator variables were extracted in original reported units; post-hoc category sensitivity will be applied where numeric recoding would impose arbitrary thresholds absent empirical justification.

Risk-of-bias assessment in the present iteration is limited to the automated quality gates embedded in the universal evidence contract. The topic pack's study designs span policy evaluations, quasi-experimental natural experiments, and economic simulation models; the appropriate per-domain quality framework for such designs is declared in the method-citation anchor list [CIT:supporting-method-anchor-placeholder]. Per-domain expert adjudication applying that framework is deferred to the pre-publication step; the present iteration relies exclusively on the automated contract gates described above.

The pipeline runs an inverse-variance random-effects pooling model over contract-passing effect estimates within a single metric family drawn from [PLACEHOLDER:preferred_metric_families]. A pooled point estimate is reported only when k ≥ 2 contract-passing effects exist within that family. Between-study variance is estimated by restricted maximum likelihood [CIT:viechtbauer2005-reml]. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus—including random intercepts for study ID and outcome-within-study, Hartung-Knapp adjustment for confidence-interval coverage [CIT:hartung2001-refined], and execution via the metafor package [CIT:viechtbauer2010-metafor]—is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5–10 contract-passing effects per metric family. Heterogeneity is reported as I² [CIT:higgins2003-inconsistency] and prediction intervals at k ≥ 2 as descriptive statistics.

Leave-one-out influence analysis, Cook's-distance influence diagnostics, funnel-plot inspection with Egger's regression test [CIT:egger1997-bias], and trim-and-fill imputation [CIT:duval2000-trimfill] are all deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 10 contract-passing effects within a metric family. Small-sample variance adjustments to confidence intervals are likewise deferred to that threshold. The translational sensitivity layer maps analog-evidence studies—those using non-carbon-tax pricing instruments—into an evidence map without pooling them into the primary corpus; this layer is reported narratively alongside the primary synthesis.

The tension matrix is a designed-for capability of the pipeline in which moderator combinations define cells—for example, tax-level stratum × jurisdiction × revenue-recycling mechanism—and the direction and magnitude of effect estimates within each cell are inspected for consistency. Sparsely populated cells containing fewer than two effects are flagged for interpretive caution rather than synthesized. The present iteration does not render the tension matrix because the corpus has not yet accrued sufficient contract-passing effects across moderator strata to populate a meaningful cell structure. This capability will be activated when the corpus accumulates k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects per metric family with adequate moderator variability to define interpretable strata.
