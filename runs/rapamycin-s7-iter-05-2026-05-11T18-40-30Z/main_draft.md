## Results

### Study Selection
Of 500 records identified through systematic database search, 500 were screened at title/abstract level; 65 were flagged as candidate records for full-text retrieval. Open-access full-text availability was located for 58 candidates; full-text content was parsed for 21 of these. Pre-specified eligibility adjudication was applied to 21 parsed records, yielding 2 included, 17 excluded, and 2 flagged for manual review of low-confidence or rule-judge conflicts for the primary pooled analysis [PACKET:study_selection].

### Corpus Characteristics
The 2 eligible studies span publication years 2021-2025 and 2 distinct venues [PACKET:corpus_characteristics].
