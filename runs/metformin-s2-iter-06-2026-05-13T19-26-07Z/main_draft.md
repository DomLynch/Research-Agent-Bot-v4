## Methods

The automated retrieval system performed a single sweep across the databases listed in the topic pack's retrieval sources, which include PubMed, Embase, Web of Science Core Collection, and Scopus [PLACEHOLDER:databases]. The executed query used a combination of population, intervention, and endpoint terms structured as (mice OR mouse) AND (metformin) AND (lifespan OR longevity OR survival OR mortality) [PLACEHOLDER:query-terms], with a date range from inception through [PLACEHOLDER:date-range]. Deduplication was performed by matching on DOI or PMID. The search and reporting strategy adheres to the framework outlined in [CIT:page-2020-prisma|method-citation].

Eligibility was determined using a two-stage automated process. First, a deterministic rule-triage pre-pass applied inclusion and exclusion criteria. The primary pooled analysis was restricted to studies in murine models examining the effect of the primary intervention, metformin, on the primary endpoint, lifespan, relative to a defined control condition [PLACEHOLDER:primary_interventions]. Studies investigating translational-only interventions (buformin, phenformin, biguanide, or biguanides) were excluded from this primary pool but retained in a separate translational sensitivity lane [PLACEHOLDER:translational_only_interventions]. Records were required to describe a primary experiment, not a review or commentary. Second, a single large language model judge adjudicated each record against the universal evidence contract, which mandates: parsed text must be adequate, the character count must exceed a minimum threshold, at least two non-title evidence quotes must be present, and the quotes must demonstrate coverage of the declared endpoint and intervention/control pairing [PLACEHOLDER:eligibility_min_text_chars]. Records failing the contract were demoted to an 'unclear' status. A subsequent strict A-core per-quote audit further required that evidence quotes affirm the study used a murine system, metformin intervention, an appropriate comparator, and assessed lifespan; non-affirming records were demoted to B-sensitivity or C-secondary lanes documented in `primary_effect_input_set_strict.json`. All adjudication decisions were filed in `eligibility_receipts.json`. There was no independent second reviewer.

Data extraction was performed by a single automated pass of the large language model on each included paper. The extraction captured the reported effect size (e.g., percent change, hazard ratio) in its original units, the sample sizes for intervention and control groups, the specific metric (e.g., median lifespan, maximum lifespan), and the exact evidence quotes supporting the numeric claim. The model name, timestamp, and all extracted values were filed as structured receipts in `effect_extractions.json`. Dual independent extraction and third-party adjudication are designed-for capabilities of the pipeline; these are deferred to the pre-publication step.

Moderator coding was conducted based on pre-specified variables drawn from the topic's scope. These include [MODERATOR_P:species_strain] (e.g., C57BL/6, C3H/Sn), [MODERATOR_P:sex] (male, female, mixed), [MODERATOR_P:age_at_treatment_start], [MODERATOR_P:dose] (in mg/kg/day or equivalent), [MODERATOR_P:route_of_administration], [MODERATOR_P:study_duration], and [MODERATOR_P:genetic_background] (wild-type, transgenic, or other). All moderators were extracted in the original reported units. Sensitivity analysis may involve post-hoc categorization of continuous moderators (e.g., dose) without imposing arbitrary, domain-irrelevant thresholds.

The automated quality screen was conducted via the universal evidence contract, which assessed parsed-text adequacy, a character-count floor, presence of at least two non-title evidence quotes, and coverage of the endpoint and intervention/comparator. For the domain of murine lifespan studies, the appropriate framework for assessing risk of bias is outlined in [CIT:hooijmans-2014-syrcle|method-citation]. Per-domain expert adjudication using this framework is deferred to the pre-publication step; the present pipeline performs only the automated rule-based screen described.

Statistical synthesis was performed for effect estimates within a single metric family from the topic pack's preferred list (e.g., percent change in median lifespan) [PLACEHOLDER:preferred_metric_families]. An inverse-variance random-effects model was applied to the set of contract-passing extractions. A pooled point estimate is reported only when k ≥ 2. The full multi-level meta-regression apparatus—including random intercepts for study and outcome, restricted maximum likelihood (REML) estimation of between-study variance, Hartung-Knapp adjustment for confidence intervals [CIT:hartung-knapp|method-citation], and computation via metafor [CIT:viechtbauer-2010-metafor|method-citation]—is a designed-for capability of the pipeline. This apparatus is deferred until the corpus accrues k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects per metric family.

Sensitivity analyses planned for when sufficient data accrue include leave-one-out diagnostics, influence measures (e.g., Cook's distance), funnel-plot inspection, Egger's regression test for small-study effects [CIT:egger-1997-funnel|method-citation], trim-and-fill, and quantification of heterogeneity via I² [CIT:higgins-2003-i2|method-citation] and prediction intervals. These are all deferred until k ≥ 10 contract-passing effects within a metric family. For translational-only interventions, a separate sensitivity layer will map analog evidence without pooling it into the primary corpus.

Tension-matrix construction is a designed-for capability of the pipeline intended to visualize effect distribution across moderator strata. Cells would be defined by combinations of moderators (e.g., male C57BL/6 mice receiving dose X). Sparsely populated cells would be flagged for caution in interpretation. The present iteration of the pipeline does not render this matrix because the corpus has not yet accrued enough contract-passing effects to populate a sufficient number of moderator-stratum cells. Execution is deferred until k ≥ 5 contract-passing effects are available across the key moderator strata.
