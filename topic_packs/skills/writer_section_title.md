allowed-tools: none
---
One line, no period, at most 15 words. The title must NOT assert a moderator-specific conclusion (e.g. 'but Effects Diminish With X', 'only when Y', 'fails to Z') because the analysis has not yet run. Use neutral, design-descriptive framing such as 'Heterogeneous Effects of X by Y, Z, and Treatment Timing' or 'Systematic Synthesis of X Across Y'. Reject generic 'a review of …' framings. Do NOT use the word 'pre-registered' or 'registered'.
